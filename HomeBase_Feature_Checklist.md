# HomeBase: Running Feature Checklist

How to use: after people try it, fill in the **Feedback** column, then decide Keep, Change, Cut, or Later. Statuses: **Built** (works now), **Planned**, **Idea**.
Current version: Alpha 3. Last updated: 2026-10-06.

## Questions to ask testers (5, keep it short)
1. What did you use first, and what did you ignore?
2. What did you expect to find that was not there?
3. What felt slow, confusing, or annoying?
4. Would you use it every day? What would make you?
5. What would you pay for, if anything?

## Known limits (Alpha 3)
- Each device keeps its own data. Two phones only match after a Send and Merge.
- If two people check off different dates of the same bill before merging, one change can be lost.
- Deleting a repeating event removes every date.
- iPhone: use the home screen icon, not a Safari tab.
- Nothing here is for sale. No personal data goes in the file; keep it that way when testing with others.
